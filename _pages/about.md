I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), working in [Prof. Gennady Pekhimenko's](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~pekhMDL/) EcoSystem research group. I am a member of [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/). My research bridges Computer Systems and Machine Learning. While my earlier work focused on computer architecture, I am currently focused on **Reliable Machine Reasoning** - specifically investigating the underlying cognitive architectures, memory boundaries, and structural limits of large language models.

Before conducting research, I served as an Intelligence Operator in the Canadian Armed Forces, where I held Top Secret security clearance and conducted intelligence analysis across all classification levels. This experience shaped the rigorous analytical mindset and discipline I bring to solving open-ended research problems today.

I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science (2022) from the University of Toronto. I also hold a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Philosophy (2011) from the University of Toronto.

## Current Project

Context Management Benchmark - investigating model's ability to maintain context over arbitrarily long math and coding reasoning chains.
